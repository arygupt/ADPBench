    How a run is scored

    Each model writes Verilog for one arithmetic operator at a time.

    1. Generate. The agent writes the design and may test it before submitting.
    2. Check. It must match the reference bit for bit on held-out cases.
    3. Synthesize. Gate cells are counted.
    4. Simulate. Clock cycles are counted.
    5. Score. Cells × cycles is the area–delay product (ADP). Lower than the baseline's wins.

    Methodology
    Beat baseline
    Runs that are correct and have a lower ADP than the baseline, out of every scheduled run. Ties go to correctness, then ADP gain.
    Correctness
    Runs that pass every check, out of every scheduled run.
    ADP gain
    Geometric mean of baseline ADP ÷ design ADP, over correct runs only.
    Failures
    Incorrect RTL counts as a failed run. Runs lost to the provider or the harness are shown as not scored but still count against the rate.
    Protocol
    Reasoning
    Each run record shows the requested reasoning setting and the share of output that was reasoning. Providers do not always honor the setting.

Metric definitions & evaluation notes

Beat baseline: correct attempts with lower cells × cycles than the baseline, divided by all attempts. Correctness: correct attempts divided by all attempts.

ADP = cells × cycles. ADP gain is the geometric mean of baseline ADP / design ADP across correct runs only. Operator bars are relative to the best correct result.

Reasoning: the share of a model's generated characters that were reasoning rather than answer text or tool-call arguments, measured from its saved responses. "Asked" is the reasoning setting the harness requested; providers do not always honor it.

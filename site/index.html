Metric definitions & evaluation notes

Beat baseline: correct attempts with lower cells × cycles than the baseline, divided by all attempts. Correctness: correct attempts divided by all attempts.

ADP = cells × cycles. ADP gain is the geometric mean of baseline ADP / design ADP across correct runs only. White lines show 95% Wilson confidence intervals. Operator bars are relative to the best correct result.

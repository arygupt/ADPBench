      Current round

      Each model works as an agent and writes Verilog for one arithmetic operator at a time. A design counts when it is correct and its area–delay product is lower than the baseline's.

      How a run is scored

      1. Generation. The agent reads the problem, writes Verilog, and may run development checks before it submits.
      2. Correctness. The frozen submission is compared with the reference bit for bit on held-out cases.
      3. Synthesis. The design is synthesized to gates, and the gate cells are counted.
      4. Simulation. The gates are simulated to count the cycles the operator takes.
      5. Score. Area–delay product is cells × cycles, compared with the baseline's.

      Primary metric: beat-baseline rate

      Beat baseline is the share of attempts that are correct and have lower cells × cycles than the baseline, out of all scheduled attempts. Correctness is the share of attempts that pass every check.

      ADP gain is the geometric mean of baseline ADP ÷ design ADP across correct runs only.

      Reasoning

      Each model's run records report the share of its generated characters that were reasoning rather than answer text or tool-call arguments, measured from its saved responses, alongside the reasoning setting the harness requested. Providers do not always honor that setting.
